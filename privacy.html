Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026 · Document version 2026-11-24.1

Screen Shield is an independent student-built project by Kabir Anand. This policy describes the Mac app and its download page. Acknowledging this policy does not waive your privacy rights.

1. Camera and screen information stays local

When enabled, Screen Shield processes webcam frames, face landmarks, face positions, and head-orientation estimates in your Mac’s memory to decide when to show a privacy cover. It does not enroll identities, create an identity database, or try to identify people by name.

When the cover is needed, the app briefly holds a screenshot in memory, blurs it, and displays the frozen image. Screen Shield does not save or transmit webcam frames, face images, or screenshots. In-memory processing is not a promise of secure memory erasure by Python or macOS.

If you open optional Model view, the app displays the live camera locally with colored face boxes and head-angle estimates for testing. This opt-in display is not recorded or transmitted. The privacy cover is suppressed while Model view is open, including during a camera outage.

2. Permissions and controls

The app requests Camera access for local detection and Screen Recording access for its in-memory screen cover. The optional global shortcut uses local keyboard events; the app does not record or upload keystrokes. Accessibility or Input Monitoring permission may be needed. You can use the app buttons if the shortcut is unavailable.

Disabling or quitting releases the webcam. Snoozing pauses processing but may keep an already-open camera allocated, so its light can remain on. You can revoke permissions through macOS System Settings.

Opening Model view while disabled temporarily enables the camera for testing. Closing it restores the previous enabled or disabled setting; if previously disabled, the camera is released. Model view continues processing during a protection snooze so you can test the model.

3. Files stored on your Mac

The app stores preferences, a small acceptance record containing the policy version, document checksum and acceptance time, and library cache data on your Mac. These are not video recordings. The packaged Mac app uses ~/Library/Application Support/Screen Shield/. The acceptance record is not sent to the developer.

Optional debug output contains numeric face sizes, head angles, states, and technical errors. The app does not install a file logger. macOS or third-party runtimes may create their own crash or diagnostic records under your system settings. Screen Shield does not automatically send those records to the developer.

If you record your own evaluation clips with another app, Screen Shield can read those local files for testing; it does not upload them or save extracted frames.

4. Download-page storage and hosting

The supplied download page contains no analytics, advertising scripts, remote fonts, accounts, or camera access. It uses browser local storage, when available, to remember acceptance of this policy and the Terms of Service. You can remove that record by clearing this site’s browser storage. If storage is unavailable, acceptance applies only to the current page session.

If this page or its downloads are hosted online, the hosting provider may receive ordinary requests, IP addresses, browser information and download metadata under that provider’s policies. That hosting traffic is separate from the local app; no camera or screen content is included. Following an external link, such as Apple’s installation guidance, is subject to that site’s practices.

5. Your choices and other people

You can decline the policies and leave the app unopened, disable detection, quit, revoke macOS permissions, or uninstall. To remove local preferences and the acceptance record after quitting, remove the Screen Shield folder in your user Application Support directory. Delete browser storage separately if desired.

Use the camera only where you have permission and consider the privacy of people nearby. Screen Shield is not designed to identify or profile children or other individuals. It has no user-account registration or intentional collection of information for the developer.

6. Changes and contact

If these documents change, their version/checksum changes and the supplied download page and app ask for acceptance again. Review a new version before accepting.

For privacy questions, contact Kabir Anand at screenshield.riverboat836@silomails.com. This public address is a forwarding alias. A support email is processed by email/forwarding providers and delivered to the developer’s private mailbox; it is not end-to-end encrypted merely because an alias is used. Do not send camera footage, screenshots, or other sensitive information unless you choose to share it.
